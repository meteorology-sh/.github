# Petrichor

Petrichor is a cloud seeding research laboratory in Austin, TX. The laboratory designs bespoke hardware and software prototypes. 

## About

### Cloud Seeding
In the 1940s, American research in aeronautics led investigators to study the physics of condensation in the cold, higher altitudes of the atmosphere. In discovering a methodology through which supercooled liquid water droplets could be coaxed together into precipitation, this research culminated in a technological advancement: cloud seeding, the technology of making rain. A cloud is seeded by various materials in the form of crystalline dust, which nucleate supercooled liquid water and precipitate from the sky.

### Natural Resources
The state of Texas has enormous water reserves in the form of surface level reservoirs, and groundwater aquifers. These water sources are commonly replenished throughout biannual rainy seasons, which present as dramatic storms. However, because of its advanced economy, and very hot climate, the state is subject to drought pressure in the dry seasons. Petrichor serves Texans from all walks of life, from industrialists to conservationists, and everyone in between.
